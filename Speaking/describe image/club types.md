# 🗣️ 一、35秒高分示范（直接背）

The bar chart illustrates the types of clubs that people belong to in the year ended March 2015.

Overall, sport clubs are the most popular, while political clubs are the least popular among all categories.

In detail, about 28 percent of people join sport clubs, followed by religion at around 21 percent and hobby clubs at approximately 15 percent. Volunteer and professional clubs are similar, both at around 11 percent. In contrast, neighbourhood and other clubs are lower, at roughly 7 percent and 5 percent respectively, while political clubs account for the smallest proportion at only about 2 percent.

In conclusion, sport and religion clubs dominate, whereas political participation remains minimal.

# 🧠 二、这类图的核心套路（必须掌握）

## ✅ 1. 开头万能句

👉 永远用：

* The bar chart illustrates…

---

## ✅ 2. Overall（最重要）

👉 一定说：

* 最大是谁（sport）
* 最小是谁（political）

👉 这是**评分重点！**

---

## ✅ 3. 不要一个一个念（低分点❌）

❌ 错误：

* Sport 28, religion 21, hobby 15…

✅ 正确（分组）：

* 高：sport + religion
* 中：hobby
* 低：其他

👉 这叫 **grouping（高分技巧）**

---

# 🔥 三、你可以套的万能模板

---

### 🧾 开头

> The bar chart illustrates ___.

---

### 🧾 Overall

> Overall, ___ is the highest, while ___ is the lowest.

---

### 🧾 细节（分组）

> In detail, the highest figures include…, followed by…
> In contrast, lower categories such as… account for…

---

### 🧾 结尾

> In conclusion, ___ dominates whereas ___ remains minimal.

---

# 🚀 四、提分关键（很多人不知道）

### ✅ 用“模糊数字”更好

👉 不要死记精确数字，用：

* about / around / approximately
* roughly

👉 更自然，机器评分更高

---

### ✅ 一定要有对比词

👉 必用：

* while
* whereas
* in contrast
* followed by

---

### ✅ 控制时间

👉 25~35秒最理想
👉 不要说完所有细节

---

# 🎯 五、一句话总结

👉 **柱状图 = 最大 + 最小 + 分组 + 对比**
