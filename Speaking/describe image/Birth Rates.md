The pie charts illustrate the birth rates of males and females for each day of the week.

Overall, the distribution of births differs slightly between males and females. For males, births are highest on Thursday and lowest on Saturday, whereas for females, births peak on Thursday and are lowest on Monday.

Specifically, for males, Thursday accounts for the largest proportion, followed by Tuesday and Monday. Wednesday and Friday have moderate shares, while Saturday and Sunday have the smallest shares. In contrast, for females, Thursday and Wednesday represent the largest portions, followed by Tuesday and Monday. Saturday has a noticeably smaller share compared with other days, while Sunday and Friday occupy moderate portions.

In summary, Thursday is the day with the highest birth rates for both genders, whereas the lowest birth rates occur on different days: Saturday for males and Monday for females.
