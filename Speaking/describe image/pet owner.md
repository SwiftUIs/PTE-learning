# 🎤 标准答案（直接背）

The image shows a pie chart illustrating the distribution across different regions.

Overall, Asia accounts for the largest proportion, while other regions such as Oceania and Europe make up smaller shares.

In detail, Asia occupies a significant part of the chart. Other regions contribute moderate to smaller percentages, indicating a clear imbalance among different areas.

In conclusion, the chart highlights that Asia dominates the distribution compared to other regions.

---

# 🧠 为什么这样说（核心技巧）

## ✅ 1. 看不清数据？没关系！

👉 你完全不需要具体数字，只要说：

* largest proportion
* smaller share
* moderate percentage

就够了 ✔

---

## ✅ 2. 饼图专用表达（必须背）

👉 高频词：

* **占比**

  * accounts for
  * makes up
* **最大**

  * the largest proportion
* **最小**

  * the smallest share
* **对比**

  * compared to

---

## ❌ 常见错误（你一定要避开）

### 🚫 错误1：一个个念区域

👉 “Asia is 30%, Europe is 20%...”

✔ 正确：
👉 只讲：

* 最大是谁
* 小的是谁
* 有没有明显差距

---

### 🚫 错误2：卡住想数据

👉 直接废掉流利度

✔ 正确：
👉 用模糊表达替代一切

# 🎯 给你一个万能口诀（饼图专用）

👉 **“最大 + 对比 + 总结”**

例如：

* 最大：Asia largest
* 对比：others smaller
* 总结：uneven distribution
