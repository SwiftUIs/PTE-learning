# 三、重点高分句型讲解 ⭐（建议背）

## 1️⃣ 让步 + 观点句（开头万能句）

**While some people argue that..., I strongly believe that...**

👉 翻译：
虽然有人认为……，但我坚信……

👉 用法（万能开头）：

* While some people argue that AI can replace teachers, I strongly believe that teachers are still essential.

✔ PTE高分关键：体现“辩证思维”

---

## 2️⃣ 不仅……而且……（高级表达）

**not only... but also...**

👉 原句：
Language is not only a tool for communication but also a carrier of culture.

👉 翻译：
语言不仅是交流工具，也是文化载体

✔ 可替换：

* is not only important for A but also for B

---

## 3️⃣ 举例句（必备）

**For example, ...**

👉 简单但必须写（不写容易低分）

✔ 升级版：

* For instance,
* A typical example is that...

---

## 4️⃣ 因果句（非常重要）

**Therefore, ...**

👉 翻译：因此

✔ 替换词：

* Thus
* As a result
* Consequently（更高级）

---

## 5️⃣ 个人经验句（高分关键⭐）

**From my own experience, ...**

👉 翻译：根据我的经验

✔ 升级：

* Based on my personal experience,
* In my own case,

👉 PTE非常喜欢这种句子（真实性+逻辑）

---

## 6️⃣ 比较/对比句（高级）

**This benefit goes far beyond what translation tools can offer.**

👉 翻译：这种好处远远超过翻译工具能提供的

✔ 结构重点：

* go far beyond（远远超过）

👉 可套用：

* This advantage goes far beyond what technology can achieve.

---

## 7️⃣ 职场常用句

**Employers often prefer candidates who...**

👉 翻译：雇主更倾向于……

✔ 万能替换：

* Universities prefer students who...
* Companies tend to hire people who...

---

## 8️⃣ 结尾句（总结）

**In conclusion, ... remains necessary for...**

👉 翻译：总之，……仍然是必要的

✔ 万能结构：

* In conclusion, A remains essential for B, C and D.
