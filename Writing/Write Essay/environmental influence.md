Nowadays, many people believe that the environment plays a crucial role in shaping an individual’s accomplishments. I strongly agree that the place where a person grows up can significantly influence their success, both positively and negatively.

Firstly, the environment determines access to educational and social resources. Individuals raised in developed areas usually have better schools, experienced teachers, and more learning opportunities. These advantages can help them build strong knowledge foundations and develop essential skills. In contrast, those who grow up in underdeveloped regions may face limited resources, which can restrict their academic and career development.

Secondly, the surrounding social environment can shape a person’s mindset and ambitions. For example, if someone grows up in a community where people value education and hard work, they are more likely to adopt similar attitudes. On the other hand, negative environments, such as those with high crime rates or low expectations, may discourage individuals from pursuing higher goals.

Finally, family environment also plays a key role. Supportive parents who encourage learning and personal growth can greatly enhance a child’s confidence and motivation, which are essential for success.

In conclusion, the environment has a profound impact on people’s accomplishments by influencing their opportunities, mindset, and support systems. Therefore, it is important to create positive environments that help individuals achieve their full potential.

# 🧠 一、题型拆解（先搞懂本质）

题目：

> Do you think the environment affects people’s accomplishment and how it affects?

👉 本质是：

### ✅ 1. 表态（agree / disagree）

### ✅ 2. 解释原因（how = 为什么 + 怎么影响）

👉 和普通agree/disagree不同的是：
**必须写“影响机制”**（不是只说好或不好）

---

# 🧩 二、万能结构（4段式，PTE最稳）

### 🟩 1️⃣ 开头（改写 + 立场）

👉 模板：

* Nowadays, it is widely believed that…
* I strongly agree / partially agree that…

---

### 🟩 2️⃣ 主体段1（外部资源）

👉 写：

* 教育资源
* 机会

👉 万能句：

* The environment determines access to resources such as education and opportunities.

---

### 🟩 3️⃣ 主体段2（内在影响）

👉 写：

* mindset（思维）
* values（价值观）
* motivation（动力）

👉 万能句：

* The surrounding environment also shapes individuals’ attitudes and ambitions.

---

### 🟩 4️⃣ 结尾（总结 + 升华）

👉 模板：

* In conclusion, the environment has a significant impact on…
* Therefore, creating a positive environment is essential.

---

# 🔥 三、这类题的核心套路（重点）

你一定要记住👇

## 👉 万能“三板斧”

几乎所有这种题都可以写：

### ① 资源（Resources）

* 学校
* 机会
* 人脉

---

### ② 思维（Mindset）

* 努力 vs 躺平
* ambition（野心）
* 自信

---

### ③ 家庭（Family）

* 支持
* 教育方式

---

👉 你写的时候 =
**资源 + mindset + family → 必出7分结构**

---

# ⚠️ 四、很多人写低分的原因

### ❌ 错误1：只写“影响”，不写“怎么影响”

❌ 错：

> environment is important

✅ 对：

> environment affects access to education and shapes people’s mindset

---

### ❌ 错误2：举例太长

PTE不需要长例子！！

👉 一句话带过即可：

* For example, people in developed cities…

---

### ❌ 错误3：句子太简单

👉 一定要用这些词（加分）：

* significantly
* play a crucial role
* have a profound impact
* in contrast
* as a result
