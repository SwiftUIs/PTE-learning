In the future, I strongly agree that people will work fewer hours at their jobs. This trend is already visible in many developed countries, and I believe it will continue as technology advances and work efficiency improves.

Firstly, automation and artificial intelligence are replacing many repetitive tasks. As a result, employees can complete the same amount of work in less time. For example, in my own experience working in an office environment, tasks that once took several hours can now be finished quickly with the help of software tools. This clearly reduces the need for long working hours.

Secondly, people are becoming more aware of work-life balance. In the past, long working hours were often associated with productivity, but now research shows that overworking actually decreases efficiency and increases stress. Many companies are already adopting flexible schedules and shorter workweeks to improve employee well-being.

However, it is also important to note that not all industries will change at the same pace. Some sectors, such as healthcare and emergency services, will still require long and irregular hours due to the nature of the work.

In conclusion, I believe that overall working hours will decrease in the future because of technological progress and changing attitudes toward work. Personally, I think this is a positive development that will allow people to enjoy a healthier and more balanced life.
