The power of friendship gets stronger with age and may even be more important than family relationships ,indicates new research by a Michigan State University scholar. In a pair of studies involving nearly 280,000
people. William Chopik found that friendships become increasingly important to one's happiness and health across the lifespan. Not only that , but in older adults, friendships are actually a stronger predictor 

of health and happiness than relationships with family memebers. "Friendships become even more important as we age" said Chopik ,assistant professor of psychology . "Keeping a few really good friendships around 
can make a world of difference for our health and well-being". So it's smart to invest in the friendships that make you happiest.
